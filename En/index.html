Welcome to Vacek Valmennus

Personal training and physiotherapy tailored to your needs

I combine coaching and physiotherapy expertise to support movement, recovery, and goal-driven training under one roof.

What I'm like as a coach

Currently, I offer individual and small-group training sessions at the gym, outdoors, at the client's home, or remotely via platforms like Teams. My clients range from beginners to seasoned fitness enthusiasts of all ages. If needed, I can also assist clients in getting to their training location.

My coaching philosophy focuses on holistic well-being: sufficient sleep, regular exercise, and proper nutrition are key components. I also emphasize the importance of slowing down, relaxation, and achieving balance between mind and body, as well as fostering a connection with nature. Clear goals help clients progress towards improved self-image and overall well-being.

A well-structured wellness program

I am committed to supporting my clients in the best possible way. My strength lies in connecting with people and fostering meaningful interactions. Each client is treated as an individual, with personalized coaching tailored to their specific needs and goals.
