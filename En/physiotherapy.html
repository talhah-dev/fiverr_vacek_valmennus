Vacek Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy that gets you moving again

I combine physiotherapy expertise with years of personal training experience to help you reduce pain and return to movement - sustainably.

Services

From pain back to movement

What I do

  • Initial assessment and evaluation

    A full review of mobility, pain drivers and functional capacity.

  • Pain management

    A focused, holistic approach to acute and persistent pain.

  • Post-surgery rehabilitation

    A safe and progressive return to daily life and exercise.

  • Sports injuries, especially for runners

    Treatment and prevention, technique analysis and return-to-sport planning.

Why this matters

Because I also come from a personal training background, rehabilitation does not stop when the pain eases. We build strength and movement control together to help prevent the problem from coming back.
